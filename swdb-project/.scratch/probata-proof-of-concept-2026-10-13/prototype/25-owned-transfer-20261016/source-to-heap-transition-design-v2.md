Created: 2026-10-16 ET. This is a concrete interpretation and proof plan, not source or member admission. It binds the unchanged original model bodies in the reviewed owned-transfer packet published at `d7f685c6fdbd7ff776eb1eaccc40099a2c48dc08`. The checked state must distinguish a reserved constructor receiver from a live constructed vector. A fresh receiver reservation has an active registry entry and no backing, but its physical fields are uninitialized. Registry backing zero therefore supplies no physical `data == NULL` fact before construction. A live receiver relates its initialized `data`, `size` and `capacity` fields to its backing identity and scalar values. The complete source-created owner set, including all automatic owners in scope, is explicit. Raw data pointers and iterators are observations, not owner registrations.

The registry frontier is the physical heap's never-issued `next`, not a count of currently live owners. The relation requires every active receiver and positive backing to be issued and live, every dynamic backing to be a freeable allocation base, distinct owner backings to have distinct allocation identities, and no positive backing identity to be any active receiver identity. Well-formed heap separation then supplies nonoverlap between those distinct live identities. This does not follow from the older unary `valid` or freeability contracts. Freed identities remain issued in history and cannot be recycled by the correspondence.

Target layout comes from an admitted compiler certificate for the actual substituted model struct. On the pinned x86_64 layout the expected fields are pointer at offset 0, size at 8 and capacity at 16, with a 24-byte, 8-aligned receiver. These numbers are a prospective equality check, not a C++ standard-library ABI assumption. The source rule must refuse a changed field, offset, width, alignment, member body or full original contract. It reuses the exact library substitution and allocator TCB, including framing, fresh valid results, freeability/release and permitted replacement effects.
