**Disposition:** retain all current bounds. Before actual17 assembly/capture, require a size-only manifest of the actual direct roots and full reference closure (plus future policy/report/summary/projection/receipt sizes), with exact existing file/ID/hash/index provenance. If a demanded original exceeds32 MiB, that establishes a concrete refusal requiring a separately reviewed narrow consumer change; do not truncate, sanitize, omit or silently expand the controls. Neither a demonstrated >32 MiB17 closure blocker nor complete future17 readiness is presently established.
